# Life in the UK Test Resources

## Knowledge

- [GOV.UK — Life in the UK Test](https://www.gov.uk/life-in-the-uk-test) (official)
  The canonical source for booking, fee (£50), test format (24 questions, 45 minutes, pass mark 18/24 = 75%), test centre locations, and what to bring on the day. This is the site the user asked to be used as the primary reference. Use for: anything about the *mechanics* of taking the test — booking rules, ID requirements, rescheduling, results.
- **"Life in the United Kingdom: A Guide for New Residents"** (Home Office, 3rd edition, publisher TSO)
  The ONLY officially sanctioned study material — every test question is drawn from this handbook, and nothing else. Five chapters: (1) The values and principles of the UK, (2) What is the UK?, (3) A long and illustrious history, (4) A modern, thriving society, (5) The UK government, the law, and your role. Use for: all factual content in every lesson — if a claim isn't traceable to this handbook, it shouldn't be taught as test content.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/ukvisa](https://reddit.com/r/ukvisa)
  Active community for UK visa/settlement applicants, including Life in the UK Test experiences. Not independently vetted for moderation quality yet (see Gaps) — useful for "what was the test actually like" anecdotes and ILR-application context, but don't treat unofficial claims about test content as authoritative; the handbook is authoritative.
