Test Mechanics Cheat Sheet

The numbers and rules of the test itself — not the content it examines. Know this cold before you even start revising facts.

The numbers

ThingValue
Questions24, multiple choice
Time limit45 minutes (~1 min 52 sec per question on average)
Pass mark18 correct out of 24 (75%)
Room for errorUp to 6 wrong and you still pass
Fee£50 per sitting
FormatComputer-based, in person, at an authorised test centre
BookingOnly via the official GOV.UK booking system, at least 3 days ahead

The one legitimate study source

"Life in the United Kingdom: A Guide for New Residents" (Home Office, 3rd edition). Every question on the test is drawn from this handbook — nothing else. Apps, question banks, and "practice tests" from third parties are not official; treat them as rehearsal, not a substitute for the handbook itself.

The handbook's five chapters

ChapterCovers
1. The values and principles of the UKDemocracy, the rule of law, individual liberty, tolerance of different faiths/beliefs, participation.
2. What is the UK?The four nations, geography, population, symbols.
3. A long and illustrious historyFrom early settlement through to the modern era — dates, monarchs, wars, key figures and events.
4. A modern, thriving societyCustoms, religion, arts, sport, leisure, everyday life.
5. The UK government, the law, and your roleHow government works, devolved administrations, the courts, voting, your responsibilities as a resident.

Chapters 2 and 4 tend to overlap most with what you already pick up from living in the UK day to day. Chapters 3 and 5 carry the highest density of specific facts, dates, and structural detail you're less likely to know just from lived experience — prioritise study time there.

This cheat sheet covers test mechanics and the study roadmap only — not the historical/factual content itself. Content-specific lessons need to be checked against an actual copy of the 3rd edition handbook for accuracy before being trusted as test prep.
