# Mission: Passing the Life in the UK Test

## Why
You're preparing to apply for Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR) in the next 1-3 months. Passing the Life in the UK Test satisfies part of the "Knowledge of Language and Life in the UK" requirement for that application — without it, the ILR application can't proceed.

## Success looks like
- Book and pass the test on the first attempt: 18/24 (75%) or better, within the 45-minute limit
- Confident, specific recall of the historical facts, dates, and figures the handbook covers — the identified weak spot, since everyday life in the UK builds cultural/practical familiarity but not the test's specific factual recall
- Working knowledge of UK government structure and legal system as the handbook presents it (devolved administrations, the courts, voting, etc.)
- Comfortable completing official-style 24-question practice tests under real time pressure, consistently scoring well clear of the pass mark before booking

## Constraints
- 1-3 month runway before the test needs to be booked and sat
- Already lives in the UK and has everyday familiarity with British culture and institutions — but hasn't systematically studied the handbook's specific facts, dates, and figures
- The test draws questions ONLY from one official source: "Life in the United Kingdom: A Guide for New Residents" (Home Office, 3rd edition). No other material is officially sanctioned — lessons must stay traceable to this handbook, not general UK trivia
- Fee is £50 per sitting, booked only through the official GOV.UK booking system — a wrong-facts study plan has a real cost if it means re-booking

## Out of scope
- English language proficiency (ESOL) — not being tested here; assumed adequate given time already living/working in the UK
- Deep academic British history beyond what the handbook covers — depth follows the handbook's own scope, not general historical curiosity
- Other parts of the ILR application (visa category rules, continuous residence calculations, documents) — this workspace is scoped to the Life in the UK Test specifically
